# AI use

I did the majority of the work on this project: I came up with the research questions, designed the methods and the study, wrote code, made the analysis decisions, and checked the results. I also used Claude (Anthropic) extensively as an AI assistant, from October 2026 onward, for writing and debugging code, finding public datasets, and drafting documents. Claude was not used to write the research plan, abstract, or poster, or to create citations.
